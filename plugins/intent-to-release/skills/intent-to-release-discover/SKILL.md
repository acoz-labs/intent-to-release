# Discover

Turn an opportunity into an executable product decision. Read [the Product Discovery phase](../../references/method.md#phase-1-product-discovery) and copy [the product brief](../../assets/templates/product-brief.md).

1. Separate evidence, assumptions, and unknowns.
2. Define audience, critical tasks, outcome, acceptance criteria, non-goals, risks, and stop signals.
3. Select the smallest coherent delivery slice.
4. Ask product authority to approve, revise, pause, or stop at **Product Authority Gate 1**.
5. Record the approver, decision, and date in the brief.

Do not design implementation or treat discussion as approval. Stop after Gate 1. If approved, hand the brief to `$intent-to-release-design`; otherwise preserve the decision and return to discovery only when evidence changes.

Output one artifact explicitly titled **Product Brief** and explicitly report
**Product Authority Gate 1** as pending, approved, revised, paused, or stopped.
The issue or equivalent product-intent artifact owns it.
